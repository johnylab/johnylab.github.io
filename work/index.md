I'm a full stack engineer with 18+ years of experience building web products used by millions, and for the last few years I've focused on shipping **AI/LLM features to production**. I'm based in Santos, SP (Brazil), work fluently in English and Portuguese, and I'm open to full-time roles and selected contract work.

## What I do best

- **Product front ends and full stack delivery:** React, Next.js and TypeScript on the front end; Node.js, Go and Java on the back end.
- **AI-powered products:** LLM integrations, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and agent-assisted workflows, from prototype to production.
- **Architecture and team leadership:** technical standards, code reviews and mentoring in distributed teams.
- **AI-assisted engineering:** daily use of Claude Code, Cursor, GitHub Copilot and Windsurf, always with review of every generated diff and tests as the safety net.

## How I work

1. **Understand the problem first.** I start from the user and the business outcome, check what already exists, and only then decide what to build (or not build).
2. **Small, verifiable steps.** Tests and clear acceptance criteria before shipping, including for AI features.
3. **Clear communication.** Short written updates, explicit trade-offs, and decisions people can follow later.
